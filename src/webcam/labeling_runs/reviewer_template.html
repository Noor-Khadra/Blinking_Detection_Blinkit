Review method
  1. Watch at normal speed, then inspect frame by frame.
  2. Adjust onset, maximum closure and offset. Keep a consistent boundary convention.
  3. Accept as a blink, reject a false proposal, or mark uncertain / sustained closure.
  4. Watch between candidates and add missed events. Face detection alone does not prove the eyelids were visible.
  5. Export the CSV. Only accepted, visually checked blink rows are verified blink labels.

Boundary convention: onset = first visibly closing frame; peak = greatest visible closure; offset = first clearly reopened frame. Frame precision is limited by the recorded video.
